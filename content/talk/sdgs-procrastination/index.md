Presented as an oral presentation in Symposium 3 of *The International Conference of the Applied Psychology in SDGs and the Annual Meetings* (當應用心理學遇上永續發展目標（SDGs）國際學術研討會暨會員大會), hosted by the Taiwan Applied Psychology Association (TAPA) and the Department of Psychology, Asia University.

The talk presented the design of a course built around procrastination — *"Psychology of Procrastination"* — grounded in self-regulated learning (SRL) training, along with an evaluation of its learning effectiveness. My contribution to this ten-author collaboration was the statistical analysis and data processing behind the effectiveness evaluation.

The underlying work was funded as an MOE (Taiwan Ministry of Education) Teaching Practice Research Program project (No. PGE1101343, PI: 林君昱, National Cheng Kung University, Dept. of Psychology; funding period 2021.08–2023.01). The full project report and the conference certificate of presentation are both available above.
